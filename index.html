About Manka

Manka Liberator is a critically acclaimed author, educator, and professional counselor with over 15 years of experience in community support and education. Based in Yaoundé, Cameroon, she has dedicated her career to fostering mental health awareness and professional excellence.

Through her leadership at Safe Home and Liberty Global Association, she integrates psychosocial support with professional training, helping individuals navigate the complexities of personal healing and career development.
